As part of my Pinternship journey with the VLED Lab at IIT Ropar, I have completed the first phase of the internship, which focuses on learning the MERN stack. Even though the internship is conducted in an online mode, the overall learning experience has been very engaging, structured, and motivating.

The learning started on the ViBe (Vikram–Betaal) platform. Instead of long lectures, the concepts were explained using short videos of around one to one and a half minutes. After each video, there was a short quiz that helped me check my understanding immediately. This method kept me focused and ensured that I did not miss important details. The AI-proctored environment also encouraged discipline and made the learning process more meaningful.

Along with ViBe learning, the live sessions and online standup meetings added great value to the internship. These sessions made the learning interactive and helped clear doubts through discussions. The online quizzes conducted during live sessions kept everyone engaged and energetic. The standup meetings created a sense of routine and responsibility, which helped me stay consistent throughout the course.

Completing this phase helped me build a strong foundation in the MERN stack and also improved my learning habits. I learned the importance of consistency, active participation, and regular practice. I have now completed one-third of the internship and am moving forward to the case studies phase. I understand that this phase will be challenging, but I feel prepared and confident to take on the case studies and apply what I have learned so far.

This learning journey has been guided and supported by @VLED Lab (VicharanaSala), IIT Ropar, under the guidance of @Dr. Sudarshan Iyengar. The structured approach, continuous support, and engaging learning environment have made this experience meaningful and valuable. I look forward to continuing this journey and learning more in the upcoming phases.

Looking forward to the next phase of learning, challenges, and growth ahead.
